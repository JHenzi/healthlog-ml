HealthLog Companion · design review · October 2026

A flat, rule-driven look for the Companion

The Companion is an analysis layer built in a fork next to HealthLog. It reads HealthLog's tables read-only and turns them into plain sentences, discoveries and a daily log. This page shows its proposed new look on the real templates, rendered with synthetic data, for comment before it ships.

Recorded day Not recorded (never zero) Accent: primary action and preliminary findings only

What changes

One typeface, three weights

Archivo at 400, 600 and 800, self-hosted in the app so no page calls out to a font service.

Rules instead of cards

Sections are separated by 2px ink rules and rows by lighter ones. Nothing floats, nothing is rounded.

Red is rationed

The accent marks the primary action, the current section and preliminary findings. Everything else is ink on a light ground.

Missing stays missing

Unlogged days are hatched in the coverage strip and stay out of every statistic. A count becomes zero only on a reviewed day.

Evidence on every claim

Each finding carries its days and strength, and is tagged Preliminary below 30 days. Wording stays associative.

Where data comes from

Every metric is tagged Imported (Apple Health via HealthLog), Logged (your trackers) or Derived (stress, energy reserve).

Screens at phone width

Live HTML from the app's own templates, scrollable inside each frame. Codes refer to the wireframe screens. Buttons and links are inert here. All values are synthetic: 90 days with three planted relationships. The insight sentences are the engine's real output on that data.

From the wireframes, not built yet

  • Evidence intervals (1b, 1c). The likely-range bar needs confidence intervals from the engine. Today it reports effect, days and adjusted p.
  • Explore (3a). Aligned timelines, scatter and calendar views with a lag setting.
  • Experiments (4a). n-of-1 observation periods with their own evidence summary.
  • Settings and data (4b). Source health, day boundary and export, plus findings from notes once the diary exists.

Questions for review

  1. Does the coverage strip make "not enough data yet" clear without feeling like a scold?
  2. Are Preliminary tags at under 30 days the right threshold, or should they follow the engine's own minimum of 21?
  3. Would any of this look carry over to HealthLog's Grafana dashboards, or should the two stay visually separate?
