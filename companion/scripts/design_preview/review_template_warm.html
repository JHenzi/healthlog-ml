HealthLog Companion · design review · October 2026

A warmer, friendlier look for the Companion

The same app and the same screens as the Modernist review, in a second look to compare: paper and soil instead of ink and red, moss green for actions, rounded tiles, a soft serif for headings. It's one extra stylesheet on top of the current one, so either can ship.

What changes from the Modernist look

Two friendly typefaces

Fraunces, a soft serif, for headings and numbers; Nunito, a rounded sans, for everything else. Both self-hosted, both open-licensed.

Tiles instead of rules

Sections sit on light tiles with rounded corners. Buttons and tags are rounded too. The hard 2px ink rules are gone.

Earth colours

Paper and oat grounds, soil-brown text, moss green for the main action and for tags that are switched on. No red.

Labels in plain case

Small labels read as normal sentence case instead of tracked capitals, so the page talks rather than announces.

Same structure

Missing days stay hatched, Preliminary and strength tags stay, ratings stay one ramp with 10 darkest. Only the skin changes.

Dark mode included

A warm dark palette: dark soil ground, cream text, a lighter moss for actions.

Screens at phone width

Live HTML from the app's own templates, scrollable inside each frame. Codes refer to the wireframe screens. Buttons and links are inert here. All values are synthetic: 90 days with three planted relationships. The insight sentences are the engine's real output on that data.

From the wireframes, not built yet

  • Evidence intervals (1b, 1c). The likely-range bar needs confidence intervals from the engine. Today it reports effect, days and adjusted p.
  • Explore (3a). Aligned timelines, scatter and calendar views with a lag setting.
  • Experiments (4a). n-of-1 observation periods with their own evidence summary.
  • Settings and data (4b). Source health, day boundary and export, plus findings from notes once the diary exists.

Questions for review

  1. Does the coverage strip make "not enough data yet" clear without feeling like a scold?
  2. Are Preliminary tags at under 30 days the right threshold, or should they follow the engine's own minimum of 21?
  3. Would any of this look carry over to HealthLog's Grafana dashboards, or should the two stay visually separate?
